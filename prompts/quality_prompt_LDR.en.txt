You are an expert in argumentation analysis. Evaluate the quality of the given argument across the following three dimensions. The rating scale is 1-5.

1. Logical / Cogency: An argument is cogent if it has acceptable premises that are relevant to its conclusion and that are sufficient to draw the conclusion.
2. Dialectical / Reasonableness: Argumentation is reasonable if it contributes to the issue’s resolution in a sufficient way that is acceptable to the target audience.
3. Rhetorical / Effectiveness: Argumentation is effective if it persuades the target audience of (or corroborates agreement with) the author’s stance on the issue.
